Tiramisu Recipe

A classic Italian dessert made with layers of coffee-soaked ladyfingers and a creamy mascarpone filling.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups heavy whipping cream , (360 ml)
  • 8 oz (225g) mascarpone cheese
  • 3 large eggs
  • 3/4 cup (150g) granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup (60ml) strong brewed coffee, cooled
  • 2 tbsp instant espresso powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 cup (50g) cocoa powder, for dusting
  • 200g ladyfingers (savoiardi)

Steps

  1. Prepare the coffee mixture by combining the strong brewed coffee, espresso powder, and vanilla extract.
  2. In a large bowl, whip the heavy cream until stiff peaks form. Set aside.
  3. In another bowl, beat the mascarpone cheese until smooth and creamy. Add the sugar and eggs, beating until well combined.
  4. Gently fold the whipped cream into the mascarpone mixture until fully incorporated.
  5. Dip each ladyfinger into the coffee mixture and arrange them in a single layer in a greased dish.
  6. Spread half of the mascarpone mixture over the ladyfingers.
  7. Arrange another layer of coffee-dipped ladyfingers on top.
  8. Spread the remaining mascarpone mixture over the top layer.
  9. Dust with cocoa powder and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight before serving.
